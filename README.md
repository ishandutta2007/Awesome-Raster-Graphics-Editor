# Awesome-Raster-Graphics-Editor

## Top Raster Graphics Editor Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Photo Editing, Digital Painting & Open-Source Image Manipulation*  

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **commercial raster graphics editors** and **open-source projects** that manipulate pixel-based images for photo retouching, digital painting, compositing, and graphic design.



**Examples** include Windows Paint, Adobe Photoshop, GIMP, Corel PaintShop Pro, Affinity Photo, Paint.NET, Krita, Pixelmator Pro, Photopea, and Sumo Paint (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Raster graphics is one of the strongest open-source domains. **GIMP**, **Krita**, **Inkscape** (vector), and **Paint.NET** collectively serve millions of artists and photographers. **PhotoGIMP** brings Photoshop-like UX to GIMP, **DigiKam** handles photo management, and **Upscayl** provides AI upscaling. **RawTherapee** and **Darktable** cover RAW processing. ## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Adobe Photoshop](https://www.adobe.com/products/photoshop.html)**  

  **The industry standard for raster graphics** — unmatched feature depth, AI-powered Generative Fill, and deep integration with Adobe ecosystem. **Subscription-based** ($22.99/month). **The reference point for all other editors** — used by every professional design studio.



- **[Affinity Photo](https://affinity.serif.com/en-us/photo/)**  

  **The best one-time-purchase Photoshop alternative** — professional features, excellent performance, and no subscription. **One-time purchase** ($69.99). **The leading commercial competitor to Photoshop** — used by professionals wanting to avoid subscriptions.



- **[Corel PaintShop Pro](https://www.paintshoppro.com/)**  

  **The veteran Windows photo editor** — affordable, feature-rich, and accessible. **One-time purchase** ($99.99). **The best budget Photoshop alternative** for Windows users.



- **[Pixelmator Pro](https://www.pixelmator.com/pro/)**  

  **The best macOS-native photo editor** — beautiful interface, ML-powered features, and deep Apple ecosystem integration. **One-time purchase** ($49.99). **The macOS standard** for professional photo editing.



- **[Photopea](https://www.photopea.com/)**  

  **The best browser-based Photoshop alternative** — free with ads, supports PSD, XD, Sketch, and more. **No installation required** — runs entirely in browser. **The most accessible Photoshop alternative** — used by students and casual users.



- **[Sumo Paint](https://sumo.app/)**  

  Browser-based image editor with layers, filters, and blending modes. **Free tier with ads**; Pro for advanced features. **Best for quick browser-based edits** .



- **[Windows Paint](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/paint)**  

  Microsoft's built-in basic image editor. **Free and pre-installed** — best for simple crops and annotations. **Not a professional tool** but universally available.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[GIMP](https://github.com/GNOME/gimp)**  

  **The GNU Image Manipulation Program — the leading open-source raster editor**, GPL-3.0 licensed with **5,000+ GitHub stars** . **Full-featured photo editing, compositing, and image authoring** — layers, masks, channels, paths, filters, and scripting (Script-Fu, Python-Fu) . **Cross-platform** (Windows, macOS, Linux) . **The de facto open-source Photoshop alternative** — 25+ years of development . **Trade-off**: UI is dated and less intuitive than Photoshop . **PhotoGIMP** patch brings Photoshop-like UX (keybindings, layout) . **Best for photographers and designers wanting full-featured editing without subscription** .



- **[Krita](https://github.com/KDE/krita)**  

  **The leading open-source digital painting application**, GPL-3.0 licensed with **7,000+ GitHub stars** . **Built for artists, illustrators, and concept artists** — 100+ brushes, stabilizers, wrap-around mode, and animation tools . **The best open-source alternative to Corel Painter and Clip Studio Paint** . **Cross-platform** (Windows, macOS, Linux, Android) . **Best for digital painters and illustrators** — not a photo editor.



- **[Paint.NET](https://github.com/paintdotnet/paint.net)**  

  **The best lightweight Windows image editor**, MIT licensed with **2,000+ GitHub stars** . **Intuitive interface with layers, effects, and plugins** — easier than GIMP for basic edits . **Windows-only** (requires .NET) . **The standard Windows image editor** for users wanting more than Paint but less than Photoshop . **Best for quick photo edits and annotations** .



- **[DigiKam](https://github.com/KDE/digikam)**  

  **The leading open-source photo management application**, GPL-2.0 licensed with **2,000+ GitHub stars** . **Not an editor** — but essential companion for managing large photo libraries . Features **face detection, geolocation, tagging, RAW processing, and batch editing** . **The best open-source alternative to Adobe Lightroom** . **Best for photographers managing thousands of images** .



- **[RawTherapee](https://github.com/RawTherapee/RawTherapee)**  

  **Professional open-source RAW photo processor**, GPL-3.0 licensed with **3,000+ GitHub stars** . **Advanced demosaicing, color management, and noise reduction** . **The best open-source alternative to Adobe Camera Raw** . **Cross-platform** . **Best for photographers shooting RAW** wanting precise control.



- **[Darktable](https://github.com/darktable-org/darktable)**  

  **Professional open-source photography workflow application**, GPL-3.0 licensed with **5,000+ GitHub stars** . **Virtual lighttable and darkroom** — RAW processing, non-destructive editing, and color management . **The leading open-source Lightroom alternative** . **Best for photographers wanting a complete RAW workflow** .



- **[Upscayl](https://github.com/upscayl/upscayl)**  

  **Free and open-source AI image upscaler**, AGPL-3.0 licensed with **30,000+ GitHub stars** . **Uses Real-ESRGAN and other models** to upscale images 4x-8x . **Cross-platform** (Windows, macOS, Linux) . **The best open-source AI upscaler** . **Best for enhancing low-resolution images** .



- **[IOPaint](https://github.com/Sanster/IOPaint)**  

  **Open-source image inpainting and object removal tool**, Apache-2.0 licensed . **AI-powered object removal** — erase unwanted elements with context-aware fill . **The best open-source alternative to Photoshop's Content-Aware Fill** . **Best for photo restoration and object removal** .



- **[Inkscape](https://github.com/inkscape/inkscape)**  

  **The leading open-source vector graphics editor** (not raster) — GPL-3.0 licensed . **SVG-native with professional vector tools** . **Included for completeness** — vector counterpart to GIMP/Krita . **Best for logo design and vector illustration** .



- **[Phatch](https://github.com/stani/phatch)**  

  **Open-source batch photo processor**, GPL-3.0 licensed . **Resize, rotate, watermark, and apply effects to multiple images** . **Best for automating repetitive image tasks** .



- **[PhotoFlare](https://github.com/PhotoFlare/photoflare)**  

  **Simple, fast open-source image editor**, GPL-3.0 licensed . **Basic editing with a clean interface** — lighter than GIMP . **Best for quick edits on low-resource systems** .



- **[LazPaint](https://github.com/bgrabitmap/lazpaint)**  

  **Open-source image editor with layer support**, GPL-3.0 licensed . **Simple and fast** — best for basic editing and pixel art . **Best for pixel art and simple edits** .



- **[PhotoGIMP](https://github.com/Diolinux/PhotoGIMP)**  

  **Patch for GIMP that makes it look and behave like Photoshop** . **Photoshop keyboard shortcuts, layout, and tool organization** . **The easiest way for Photoshop users to migrate to GIMP** . **Best for users wanting Photoshop UX in open-source** .

**Frameworks for building custom raster graphics solutions**: Choose based on use case. **GIMP** for full-featured photo editing and compositing with scripting . **Krita** for digital painting and illustration . **Paint.NET** for lightweight Windows editing . **Darktable** or **RawTherapee** for RAW photo workflows . **DigiKam** for photo library management . **Upscayl** for AI upscaling . **IOPaint** for object removal and inpainting . **PhotoGIMP** to make GIMP feel like Photoshop . Note that true professional raster editing with Adobe's Generative AI, cloud collaboration, and industry-standard PSD compatibility remains primarily commercial territory; open-source stacks provide strong editing, painting, and RAW processing foundations that require integration for complete creative workflows.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Raster graphics editors handle potentially sensitive images. **AI features may transmit images to external services** — review privacy settings before processing sensitive content.

- **GIMP and Photoshop have different workflows** — GIMP is not a Photoshop clone. Expect a learning curve, or use **PhotoGIMP** for Photoshop-like UX .

- **Krita is for painting, not photo editing** — it lacks advanced photo retouching tools. Use GIMP or Darktable for photos .

- **RAW processing requires specific tools** — Darktable and RawTherapee are for RAW files; GIMP and Krita work with processed images .

- The open-source ecosystem provides strong editing, painting, and RAW processing foundations, but **Adobe's Generative AI, cloud collaboration, and industry-standard PSD compatibility** remain primarily commercial offerings.
